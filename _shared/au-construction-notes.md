# AU construction notes (load only if the task needs a contract family)

This is a prompt aid, not legal advice. Prefer the project's actual contract extract over this file.

## Common contract families (Australia)

- AS 4000 — construct-only, superintendent administers, variations + EOT + latent conditions well defined.
- AS 2124 — older construct-only cousin. Clause numbers differ. Do not mix AS 4000 numbers into an AS 2124 job.
- AS 4902 — design and construct.
- ABIC SW / MW — used with architects; notices and time bars are sharp.
- GC21 — NSW government. Different notice and variation machinery.
- MBA / HIA residential — different again. Do not apply commercial notice periods.

If the contract form is unknown, write `Contract form: UNKNOWN` and use generic headings (Cause, Entitlement pathway, Time, Money, Records). Do not cite a clause number.

## RFI vs variation vs NCR

- RFI — information gap. Does not change price or time by itself.
- Variation / VO — change to WUC instructed or claimed.
- EOT — time only (may travel with cost under delay damages / delay costs — keep separate).
- NCR — work not to spec / drawing / ITP. Disposition first, money later.

Never dress a variation as an RFI.

## Evidence that survives a dispute

Dates, drawing revisions, spec clauses as written, photos with location and time, labour/plant counts from the diary, notices in the contract form, programme impacted activities by ID.

## Hold points

Typical: set-out, reinforcement / stressing, damp-proof / membrane, pre-pour, hold-down, fire-stopping, waterproofing, services first-fix before lining, commissioning. Always prefer the project ITP over this list.
